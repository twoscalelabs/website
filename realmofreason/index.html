Realm of Reason

The Math Fantasy Card Game

Realm of Reason is a card game where players apply mathematical ideas in real and imaginative scenarios. Players use ingenuity to connect concepts, test arguments, and solve problems with fantastical twists.

Mathematical connections, arguments and twists.

Realm of Reason supports discussion, decision-making, and mathematical reasoning in a format that is easy to learn and engaging to play. Designed for students, families, and educators, Realm of Reason connects problem-solving skills with foundational concepts in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

The gameplay aligns with the NY State Common Core Standards and supports the application of mathematics emphasized in IB MYP Criterion D.
